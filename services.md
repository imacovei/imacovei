# Services

We provide a complete set of services to help manufacturers, EMS companies, and integrators deploy reliable, high‑performance end‑of‑line test systems.  
Our goal is simple: ensure your production line runs smoothly, efficiently, and with full confidence in every device that leaves the factory.

---

## Integration Services

### Turnkey Test System Integration
We support the full integration of the test software into your production environment, including:
- Multi‑device parallel execution setup  
- Hardware integration (microphones, sensors, infrared thermometers, LED analyzers)  
- Fixture and tooling alignment  
- Operator workflow design  

### Custom Test Sequence Development
We design and implement test sequences tailored to your product’s functional and regulatory requirements:
- Thermal profiles  
- LED behavior and timing  
- Acoustic FFT analysis  
- Firmware flashing and validation  
- Safety and compliance checks
